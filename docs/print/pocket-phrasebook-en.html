Etiquette in brief

  • Greeting the right way. Always greet before anything else, even in a shop or an office. Shake hands with your right hand; with someone of the opposite sex, wait for them to offer theirs.
  • Ramadan and prayer. During Ramadan, avoid eating, drinking or smoking in public in the daytime. Don't walk in front of someone who is praying, and dress modestly near mosques.
  • Being a good guest. Meals are often shared from one big bowl: wash your hands, eat with your right hand from the part in front of you, and let the host share out the meat.
