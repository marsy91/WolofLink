# Pitch email for organisations

Send from support@woloflink.com, attach `WolofLink-for-organisations.pdf`.
Personalise the first line every time; send 5–10 a day, not 100 at once.

**Subject:** Wolof for your team in Senegal: free 1-month pilot

Hello [first name],

I'm [your name], founder of WolofLink, an app that teaches Senegalese Wolof in
short daily lessons. I'm writing because [organisation] [has staff / volunteers /
students] in Senegal, and a few words of Wolof make a real difference to how
people are welcomed there.

WolofLink gives your team:

- 20 units of everyday Wolof, plus a 100-phrase survival kit for the first weeks
- culture and etiquette lessons, and a calendar of Senegalese holidays
- audio for every line, and it works fully offline
- one team code to join, and a dashboard where you see progress (never emails)

I'd like to offer [organisation] a free one-month pilot for up to 10 people.
There's nothing to install on your side: I send you a code, your team enters it
in the app, and you can follow their progress online.

Would that be useful? If so, just reply with roughly how many people you'd like
to include.

Jërëjëf (thank you),
[your name]
WolofLink · woloflink.com

**Subject:** Re: Wolof for your team in Senegal

Hello [first name], just bringing this back to the top of your inbox. If Wolof

[your name]

## Who to send it to

- Embassies and consulates in Dakar: the cultural or administration attaché
- NGOs and volunteer programmes working in Senegal: programme or HR managers
- International schools in Dakar: head of languages
- Universities with study-abroad programmes in Senegal
- Companies with staff in Senegal (mining, energy, telecoms, logistics)
