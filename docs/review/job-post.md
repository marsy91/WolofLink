# Job post: Senegalese Wolof reviewer

Ready to paste into Upwork, Fiverr or italki. Post it twice and hire two
people: the second one checks anything the first one changed.

**Title:** Native Senegalese Wolof speaker to review a language-learning app (text + audio)

**Description:**

I'm launching WolofLink, an app that teaches Senegalese Wolof to English and
French speakers (travellers, NGO and embassy staff, the diaspora). I need a
native speaker from Senegal, ideally from Dakar, to check the Wolof before
launch.

What you'll do:

- Go through a spreadsheet of about 630 Wolof words, phrases and short
  dialogue lines, each with its English and French meaning.
- For each line, mark OK or Fix for: spelling/wording, meaning, and audio
  (each line has a short audio clip to play).
- When something is wrong, write the correct version, or what people really
  say in Dakar.
- Check 15 short descriptions of Senegalese holidays (Tabaski, Korité,
  Magal, Gamou…) for accuracy.
- About 150 lines are marked "check first". Please send those back first.

You should:

- Be a native Wolof speaker who grew up in Senegal (not The Gambia; spelling
  and some words differ).
- Write Wolof in standard CLAD spelling (ë, é, à, ñ, ŋ, double vowels).
- Read English or French comfortably.

Estimated time: 8 to 15 hours. Paid test task first: the first 30 lines.

To apply, translate these three phrases into Wolof as you'd say them in
Dakar: "Where is the bus station?", "I don't feel well", "Happy holiday,
forgive me my wrongs".

**Budget:** fixed price, or hourly. Please give your rate.

**Terms:** you keep the work confidential until launch. Corrections become
part of the app; you can be credited by name in the app's About section if
you wish.
