## English (en-US / en-GB)

**App name:** WolofLink: Learn Wolof

**Short description:**
Learn Wolof, Senegal's language: short lessons, audio, phrases. Works offline.

**Full description:**
Learn Wolof the way Senegal speaks it.

WolofLink teaches Wolof, the most widely spoken language of Senegal, in short, friendly lessons. Learn in English, French, Spanish, German or Italian, and practise a few minutes a day, even without internet.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 20 units and 53 lessons, from greetings to market prices, food, health, travel and feelings
• 410 words and phrases, each with audio and a slow-play button
• Pictures, listening, building sentences and typing; every lesson replays at three levels
• A dictionary of 440+ words and phrases: search in Wolof or your own language, accents optional
• Survival Wolof: the 100 words and phrases to know first, with a quick practice mode
• Culture and etiquette: greeting the right way, Ramadan and prayer, being a good guest
• Spaced-repetition review of the words you find hard
• Holidays in Senegal: Tabaski, Korité, the Magal and more, with what to expect, the right greeting and an optional reminder
• Spot a mistake? Report it from any word in one tap
• Seasonal themes for Ramadan, Tabaski, Korité, Independence Day and more

STAY MOTIVATED
• Daily goal, streak and badges
• Optional daily reminder
• Classic theme or the colours of the Senegalese flag

FOR TEAMS AND ORGANISATIONS
NGOs, embassies, schools and companies can give their staff a team code. Staff join from the app and choose the name their organisation sees; administrators see a progress summary, never emails.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and no tracking. Works fully offline without an account. An optional account syncs your progress between devices.

Whether you're visiting Senegal, working there, reconnecting with family or simply curious: Dalal ak jàmm, welcome!
