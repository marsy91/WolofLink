# Tester kit: closed test on Google Play

Everything you need to recruit 12 testers, keep them in for 14 days and collect useful feedback.
Messages are in English and French; copy and edit.

## 1. Who to ask
Google needs **at least 12 testers with a Google account** (Gmail) who stay opted in for **14 days in a row**.
Aim for **16–20 people**: some will forget to install or will drop out.

Good testers:
- Friends and family with Android phones (any age).
- Senegalese and Gambian people in your network, and Wolof speakers if you can: their feedback on the Wolof is worth gold.
- Language learners, travellers to Senegal, aid and NGO workers.
- Mix of phones: older and cheaper ones matter most.

## 2. Collect their Gmail addresses
Play Console → your app → **Test and release → Testing → Closed testing → Testers → Create email list** → paste the Gmail addresses.
(They must be the Google account that is signed in on their phone's Play Store.)

## 3. The invite (send after you start the rollout)
Copy the **opt-in link** (Testers tab → "Copy link") into this message.

**WhatsApp / email (English)**
> Hi [name], I've built a free app for learning Wolof (Senegal's main language) and I need 12 people to test it for 2 weeks before it goes public. It takes 5 minutes to set up and then just a few minutes of use whenever you like.
>
> 1. On your Android phone, open this link and tap **Become a tester**: [LINK]
> 2. Then tap the **Download it on Google Play** link on that page and install **WolofLink**.
> 3. Please keep it installed for the next 14 days (don't uninstall or leave the test) and use it a few times. That's what Google needs from us.
>
> When you try it, tell me anything that's confusing, wrong or broken: there's a **Report a problem** button on every word, or just reply to me. Thank you so much!

## 4. What to ask them to try (one-page checklist for testers)
1. Open the app, choose your language and start a lesson (Greetings).
2. Play a lesson to the end. Did the sounds play? Did anything feel too hard, too easy or wrong?
3. **Practice → Listen hands-free**: pick a unit and press Play.
4. **Practice → Role-play**: finish a lesson with a dialogue (unit 1), then play a part.
5. Speaking exercise: record yourself and play it back next to the model voice.
6. **Dictionary**: search a word in Wolof and in your language.
7. **Profile → My progress**, and **Settings → Appearance** (try dark mode).
8. Turn off the internet and open the app: it should still work.
9. If you speak Wolof: look at 10 words and tell us anything that sounds or reads wrong.
10. **Profile → Send feedback**: tell us one thing you liked and one thing to improve (it also works offline).
11. If friends and duels are switched on: add another tester as a friend (Friends → Add in person, QR code) and play a duel.

## 5. How they report problems
- **In the app:** tap *Report a problem* on a word (spelling, translation, audio), or use
  **Profile → Send feedback** for anything else. Both arrive in the owner console (Reports).
- **Anything else:** reply to your message or email support@woloflink.com.
- Useful details: phone model, what they tapped, what they expected, a screenshot.

## 6. Keep them in for 14 days
- Day 0: send the invite. Day 2: remind those who haven't joined.
- Day 7: a short thank-you and a question ("anything annoying yet?").
- Day 12: remind everyone to **stay opted in** until you say thank you.
- Check **Testing → Closed testing → Testers** for the count. Google's page shows how many are opted in; you need 12 for 14 consecutive days.
- If someone leaves, invite a replacement. Google's exact counting rules can change, so follow what the Dashboard says about your progress.

## 7. What to write down (for the production application)
Google asks how testers engaged and what you changed. Keep a running list:
- Number of testers, countries, phone types.
- 3–5 real problems testers found and what you did about them (e.g. spelling fixes, a confusing screen).
- Any native-speaker corrections.

Draft answers are in `docs/store/play-console-answers.md` (section 6). Replace the examples with these real items.

## 8. After the 14 days
Play Console → Dashboard → **Apply for production** → paste your answers → create the production release with the latest bundle (a higher version number than the test) → choose countries → roll out. Start at a small percentage (for example 20%) and raise it after a few days if there are no crashes.
